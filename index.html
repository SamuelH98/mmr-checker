Ranked MMR is derived from your visible rank, LP, and win rate. Normal-queue MMR is estimated by averaging the ranked MMRs of all 9 players in each lobby — the matchmaker groups players by similar normal MMR, making lobby average the best available proxy. Win-rate fine-tunes both estimates. Riot does not expose raw MMR values.
